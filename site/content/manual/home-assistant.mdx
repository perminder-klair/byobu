Home Assistant is a plugin, and on by default once it has an address and a token. It brings your lights, switches, fans, climate, covers, locks, media players, cameras, scenes and scripts onto the panel, along with Home Assistant's weather and calendars.

## Connecting it

Create a long-lived access token: open your Home Assistant profile, go to the Security tab, and find Long-lived access tokens. A user just for the panel keeps it separate from your own login.

Then either put `ha_url` and `ha_token` in [`byobu.conf`](/manual/settings-file/), or open Settings, then Home Assistant, on [your phone](/manual/your-phone/), which is easier than typing a token on the panel.

The token stays in byobud and never reaches a browser.

## Rooms

Rooms are your Home Assistant areas, in the same order. A device's area decides where its tiles go, and an entity's own area overrides its device's. Anything without an area is listed under Other.

Entities you've hidden in Home Assistant don't appear, and neither do its configuration and diagnostic entities.

## What each thing does

| Kind | A tap | Press and hold |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Light | On or off | Brightness, colour temperature and colour |
| Switch | On or off | On and off |
| Fan | On or off | Speed, mode, oscillation and direction |
| Climate | Opens its sheet | Target temperature, modes and presets |
| Blind | Open or close | Position and tilt |
| Garage door or gate | Opens its sheet while closed | A separate Open button |
| Lock | Locks it. Opens its sheet while locked | A separate Unlock button |
| Media player | Play or pause | Controls, volume and source |
| Scene or script | Runs it | |

A running thermostat also shows plus and minus buttons on its tile.

## Weather and calendars

If you have a weather integration, Home Assistant provides the weather, preferring `weather.forecast_home`. Calendars show the next seven days. You can also get either [without Home Assistant](/manual/weather-and-calendars/).

## If it stops working

The plugin reconnects by itself. A problem with the address or token shows on the panel and in Settings. A new token can go in `byobu.conf` or Settings at any time.
