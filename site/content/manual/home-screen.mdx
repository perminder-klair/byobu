Swipe up from any face for the home screen: apps, widgets beside them, and a rail down the side that's always one tap from home.

<Shot name="home" title="home screen" number={1} focused alt="The home screen: a rail of recent apps, widgets for music, weather and the next event, and a grid of apps." />

## Apps

An app only appears once something's there to fill it, so a home without cameras has no cameras app. Settings is always there.

| App | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| Home | Your rooms and their tiles |
| Music | Everything that can play |
| Calendar | What's coming up |
| Cameras | Your cameras, and a tap makes one full screen |
| Timers | Running timers, and [ones to start](/manual/timers/) |
| Weather | Now, the next hours and the week |
| Scenes | Scenes and scripts from Home Assistant |
| Settings | The panel, its theme and faces, phones, and each plugin |

The rail keeps your last few apps and a button that goes home. Whatever app is open, the bar and rail stay put.

## Widgets

The widgets show what's playing, the weather and what's next, and a running timer if there is one. Tap one to go to it. With the assistant set up, a prompt runs along the bottom.

## Tiles

<Shot name="rooms" title="home" number={2} alt="The Home app: a list of rooms, and tiles for the Living Room's heating, lights, blind and fan." />

**Tap** a tile to switch a light, a switch or a fan, play or pause, or open or close a blind.

**Press and hold** any tile for the rest: brightness and colour, fan speed, thermostat modes, blind position, volume.

<Shot name="sheet-light" title="press and hold" number={3} alt="A light's sheet, with brightness, colour temperature and colour sliders." />

A locked door or a closed garage doesn't do anything on a tap, as that could happen by brushing past. A tap opens its sheet, and unlocking or opening is a separate press there.

<Shot name="sheet-lock" title="front door" number={4} alt="The front door's sheet, with separate Lock and Unlock buttons." />

## The panel's own controls

The icon at the right of the bar sets volume, brightness, night mode and when the screen turns off. The same controls are in Settings, under Display.
