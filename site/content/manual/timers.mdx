Timers are on the home screen. Tap one to start it: 1, 3, 5, 10, 15, 30 or 45 minutes, or an hour. Or ask the [assistant](/manual/assistant/) for any length up to a day, with a name, like "ten minutes for the pasta".

<Shot name="timers" title="timers" number={1} focused alt="The Timers app: a Pasta timer and an Oven timer running, above buttons to start more." />

A running timer counts down in the bar, wherever you are. Add a minute or cancel it from the Timers app.

When it ends it wakes the screen and chimes until you dismiss it, for up to two minutes of chiming. The message stays until it's dismissed.

<Shot name="alert" title="finished" number={2} alt="A Timer finished message over Now Playing, with a Dismiss button." />

Timers carry on through a restart. If byobud restarted in between, it still ends on time.
