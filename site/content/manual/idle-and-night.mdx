A wall display spends most of its life with nobody touching it, so it behaves differently as the room goes quiet.

| After | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 45 seconds | Whatever was open closes, and it shows the far view |
| 5 minutes | The screen dims |
| 30 minutes | The screen turns off |

Change the last one in the panel controls, to 15, 30 or 60 minutes or never.

## The far view

The far view is one or two big windows that read from across the room. It goes to Now Playing if there's music, and otherwise to your first face. Swiping from the far view flips through the faces and stays far.

## Waking it

A tap brings it back. That tap only wakes it, so it doesn't press whatever was under your finger.

Anything that needs you wakes the screen too, like a [timer](/manual/timers/) finishing or the [wake phrase](/manual/voice/).

## Night mode

Night mode turns everything red on black at low brightness, so a screen in a dark room doesn't light it up. By default it runs from 22:00 to 07:00. You can also turn it on or off for good in the panel controls.
