The panel doesn't need Home Assistant for the weather or your calendar. These two plugins are off until you turn them on in Settings.

## Open-Meteo weather

Weather from [open-meteo.com](https://open-meteo.com), with no account needed. Open Settings, then Open-Meteo weather, turn it on, and fill in:

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Latitude | Like `51.51` |
| Longitude | Like `-0.13` |
| Temperature | Celsius or Fahrenheit |

If another plugin also provides weather, the first in Settings wins. It refreshes every 15 minutes.

## Calendar link

Upcoming events from a calendar's private `.ics` link, as Google Calendar, Apple Calendar and Fastmail can share. Open Settings, then Calendar link, turn it on, and paste in the address. `webcal://` links work too.

It shows the next 14 days and refreshes every 15 minutes.

Anyone with that link can read the calendar, so it's kept like a password: it stays in byobud and never reaches a browser. Paste it from [your phone](/manual/your-phone/) rather than typing it on the panel.

## Where they show

Weather appears on Modular, on the Weather and Clock faces and in the Weather app. Events appear in the Calendar app, on the Clock face, on Modular's next window and in the home screen's widget.
