The panel serves its own phone app, so there's nothing to install. It has your rooms and tiles, Now Playing and timers, the panel's volume, brightness, theme and faces, and every plugin's settings, with your own keyboard for long things like tokens.

## Pairing

On the panel, open Settings, then Phones, then Add a phone, and scan the code with your phone's camera. The phone must be on the same network as the panel.

Add it to your home screen from the share menu to keep it like an app.

A code works once, for five minutes, and only the panel can make one, so getting in takes standing in front of it.

## What's on it

| Tab | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| Home | Rooms and tiles, as on the panel |
| Ask | The [assistant](/manual/assistant/), if it's set up |
| Now | What's playing, and timers |
| Panel | Volume, brightness, theme and faces, and showing a face on the panel |
| Settings | Every plugin's settings |

Press and hold a tile for its controls, the same as on the panel.

## Removing one

Remove a phone in Settings, under Phones, and it's locked out at once.

## Is it private?

It's plain HTTP on your home network, so anyone already on your Wi-Fi who's watching the traffic could read what a phone sends, including a token typed into Settings. Don't forward the panel's port to the internet. [Security](/manual/security/) has more.
