Sound comes out of the 3.5 mm jack, or a USB speaker, which takes over when it's plugged in. With more than one to choose from, the panel controls ask which to use.

Volume is in the panel controls, and on [your phone](/manual/your-phone/).

## AirPlay and Spotify

The panel shows up in AirPlay and Spotify as Byobu, and what's playing appears on [Now Playing](/manual/faces/). Rename it in Settings, under AirPlay and Spotify, where you can also turn either one off.

Spotify Connect needs a Premium account on the phone. Nothing about your account is stored on the panel.

<Note>

These receivers haven't been tried with real streams yet.

</Note>

## Other players

Anything Home Assistant can play also shows up, as tiles in the Music app. They're controlled from the panel, but the sound comes out wherever that player is.
